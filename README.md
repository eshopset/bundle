## Why merchants choose EShopSet

### Save on app subscriptions
Most stores end up with a stack of single-purpose tools — separate installs, dashboards, and invoices for SEO, AI visibility, sheet sync, linking, and content. **Starter from $49/store/mo** includes the full five-app set, so you pay one plan instead of five point-solution subscriptions.

### One app to manage
A single EShopSet install gives you a marketplace-style experience: enable only what each store needs, then work from **Overview**, **Settings**, and **Logs** per app — without juggling five Admin embeds.

### Clear per-store billing
Plans are billed **per Shopify store, per month**. Multi-store portfolios pick a plan per store; app enables, usage, and logs never mix across stores — finance and ops stay aligned.

### AI Power built in
Operate the same apps from **Shopify Sidekick** in Admin, or connect **EShopSet MCP** to Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Windsurf, and more. Ask for SEO briefs, citation gaps, sync status, and next steps in plain language — without opening another dashboard.

### Built for Shopify daily ops
Designed for store operators (single shops and multi-store portfolios) and for agencies managing client stores. Agencies and app vendors can also join the [Partner Program](https://eshopset.com/partner-program/) to earn on referrals.

## Benefits by job-to-be-done

### SEO & organic growth
- Track **domain trust**, traffic trends, **keyword positions**, backlinks, and **competitor overlap** in one Overview.
- Daily sync cadence so “what changed?” is answerable without hopping between SEO suites.
- Transparent limits on tracked keywords and competitors by plan.

### AI search & brand visibility (AEO / GEO)
- Monitor citations across major AI answer surfaces — not just classic search.
- Spot citation gaps and competing domains before they hit revenue.
- Keep AEO status in your agent workflow via MCP prompts.

### Catalog operations
- Maintain products from a familiar **Google Sheet** with preview safety before apply.
- Match keys that fit real catalogs (SKU, handle, ID, barcode).
- Scheduled sync plus Logs for failed rows and run history.

### Internal linking & content
- Discover underlinked pages and apply link suggestions with control.
- Turn opportunities into drafts and publish with approval — blogs and PDP copy in one bundle.
- Credit/usage transparency so content spend stays predictable.

### Team & agency workflows
- **Overview / Settings / Logs** consistency across every enabled app.
- Per-store plans for portfolios — no shared multi-store usage bucket.
- Partner Program for agencies and vendors who refer merchants.
