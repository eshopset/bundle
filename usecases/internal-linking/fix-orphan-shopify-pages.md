## Why orphan pages hurt Shopify SEO

Search engines and AI crawlers follow links in page HTML. A product that only appears in a collection grid or theme menu—but never in body copy—is easy to under-discover. Orphan or near-orphan pages also waste content investment: blog posts and guides never pass equity to the SKUs they describe.

Link Pilot’s orphan list is based on **body HTML** edges (description and article body), not theme navigation. That matches how contextual internal linking actually works for SEO.

## How it works in Link Pilot

After index + audit, **Overview → Orphans** lists resources with little inbound body linking. The header **Score** starts at 100 and deducts **35%** for orphan rate (in-degree 0 in body HTML). Suggestion generation runs **orphan inbound** rules in parallel with **AI embeddings** (same-locale cosine neighbors). You review each card’s **Confidence** (weighted score) and AI-written Why, edit the anchor if needed (must exist in the source copy), then approve.

- Free: audit, review, DIY export, 50 welcome credits
- Optional Link Automation: faster re-checks and digests

## Example: seasonal collection with no blog paths

A footwear store launches a Winter Boots collection. Collection and product descriptions exist, but no blog or guide links into them. Overview Score sits at **61%** with a high orphan deduction; 12 SKUs plus the collection show as orphans.

The merchant generates suggestions, approves 18 orphan-inbound links (typical Confidence **72–86%**) from sizing guides and care articles using welcome credits, then re-audits. Orphan count drops, Score rises, and organic entry pages for winter queries start including the collection URL in Search Console.

## Playbook

1. Install Link Pilot and complete the store scan.
2. Open **Overview → Orphans**; export CSV if you need a spreadsheet triage.
3. Prioritize money pages (collections and top products) over policy/utility URLs.
4. Run **Generate suggestions** if the queue is empty.
5. In **Suggestions**, filter orphan-inbound types; edit awkward anchors.
6. Approve a batch and apply with credits—or DIY export into the Shopify editor.
7. Re-run audit after applies to confirm inbound counts moved.

## What to measure

- Orphan count before vs after the batch
- Inbound body links to top 20 commercial URLs
- Search Console impressions/clicks on previously orphan URLs over 14–28 days
