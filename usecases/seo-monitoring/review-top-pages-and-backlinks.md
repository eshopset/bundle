## Outcomes

- Focus on the 10–50 pages that carry most organic demand
- Pair on-page fixes with backlink opportunities
- Turn sync output into a ranked backlog, not a spreadsheet dump

## Why top pages beat keyword lists alone

Keyword tables tell you what queries moved. Top pages tell you which URLs to edit. On Shopify, that usually means a handful of collections, blog guides, and hero PDPs—not 400 variant URLs. Backlink signals then show whether authority is supporting those same URLs or leaking to thin pages.

## How it works in EShopSet

After sync, open top organic pages (limit by plan) and backlink-related signals in SEO Performance Monitor. Note URLs that appear in both “high traffic” and “ranking opportunity” sets. Put those first in your content calendar.

## Example: outdoor gear catalog

An outdoor brand on Growth saw 20 top pages; 4 collections drove ~55% of organic clicks. Backlink signals clustered on an old blog post that no longer linked to those collections. They rebuilt the post with 8 contextual links and updated 2 collection intros. Over three weeks, those 4 collections gained 12 positions across 19 tracked keywords.

## Playbook

1. Export or note the current top pages list post-sync.
2. Mark Shopify template type: collection, product, blog, other.
3. For each top collection/product, list 3 supporting internal links to add.
4. Check whether backlink equity lands on money URLs or orphans.
5. Ship ≤5 URL-level tasks per week; re-check after 7 daily syncs.

## What to measure

- % of organic clicks in top 10 pages
- Backlinks pointing to money URLs vs utility pages
- Keyword coverage per top URL (are heroes under-supported?)

## Proof points

- **10 / 20 / 50** — Top pages by plan
- **80/20** — Typical click concentration
- **Daily** — Fresh signals
