## Outcomes

- Compress a week of daily syncs into a 20–30 minute decision meeting
- Spot ±3 position swings before they become traffic cliffs
- Tie top-page lists to merchandising and content owners

## Why weekly, not only daily

Daily Overview is for anomalies (sync errors, sudden competitor spikes). Weekly review is for prioritization. Ranking noise on any single day can be 1–2 positions; a sustained −4 to −8 move across a cluster usually means a SERP feature change, a competitor content push, or an on-site regression after a theme/app deploy.

## How it works in EShopSet

Open SEO Performance Monitor → Overview after the Monday (or your team’s) sync. Scan keyword position changes, open the competitors table for overlap, and review top organic pages. Cross-check Logs if any sync was partial. Keep the meeting artifact light: 3 wins, 3 risks, 1 experiment for the week.

## Example: beauty brand ranking board

A beauty Shopify store on **Pro** tracks 86 keywords. In one weekly review they found:

- 9 keywords dropped 4+ positions (mostly “best … for sensitive skin” comparisons)
- 2 competitors newly ranking on 14 overlapping terms
- Top 20 pages still drove ~78% of organic clicks—so effort stayed on those URLs, not the long tail

They shipped 3 comparison modules and 12 internal links; two weeks later 6 of the 9 recovered into the top 10.

## Playbook

1. Filter keywords with ≥3 position change week-over-week.
2. Group by collection/theme (don’t treat 40 URLs as 40 tickets).
3. Check competitor table for domains gaining on those themes.
4. Open top pages: which URLs own the recovering/losing clusters?
5. Assign ≤3 content or merchandising tasks with owners and due dates.
6. Note the baseline date so next week’s review has a clean delta.

## What to measure

- % of tracked keywords improved / declined / flat
- Competitor count sharing ≥10 of your keywords
- Organic clicks concentrated in top 10 / 20 / 50 pages

## Proof points

- **7 days** — Review cadence
- **±3 pos** — Worth investigating
- **10–50** — Top pages by plan
