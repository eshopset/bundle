## Outcomes

- First sync establishes trust, traffic, and ranking baselines for your Shopify domain
- Track 25 / 50 / 100 keywords on Starter / Growth / Pro
- Competitor slots: 5 / 10 / 20 so peer sets stay intentional

## Why baselining comes first

Shopify catalogs change weekly—new collections, metafield edits, theme swaps. Without a dated baseline, every ranking move looks like noise. A first sync freezes domain trust, organic/paid traffic mixes, keyword positions, and backlink signals so the next 7–14 days of Overview KPIs mean something.

Teams that skip this step often chase a single keyword drop while missing that three competitors gained 2–4 positions on the same cluster.

## How it works in EShopSet

In **Settings**, set your primary Shopify domain, locale/market, and keyword/competitor limits for your plan. Save, then run the first sync from Overview. SEO Performance Monitor pulls domain-level trust and traffic, keyword positions, top pages (10 / 20 / 50 by plan), and backlink signals into tables you can revisit daily.

- **Starter:** 25 keywords · 5 competitors · 10 top pages
- **Growth:** 50 keywords · 10 competitors · 20 top pages
- **Pro:** 100 keywords · 20 competitors · 50 top pages

## Example: apparel store launch week

A DTC apparel brand on **Growth ($99/mo launch)** tracks 42 commercial keywords (collection + product intent), 8 competitors, and reviews the top 20 organic pages after the first sync.

Day 0 baseline: domain trust score recorded, US organic share at ~61% of sessions in the traffic-by-country view, and 11 of 42 keywords sitting positions 11–20. That gives the merchandising team a concrete 2-week goal: move 5 of those 11 into the top 10 after collection copy and internal-link fixes—not a vague “improve SEO.”

## Playbook

1. Install the bundle and open SEO Performance Monitor → **Settings**.
2. Confirm domain (apex or www consistently), primary locale, and plan limits.
3. Seed the first 15–25 keywords: brand + top collections + 5–8 money product queries.
4. Add 3–5 direct Shopify competitors you actually lose auctions/organic share to.
5. Run the first sync; screenshot or note Overview KPIs for the date.
6. Schedule a 15-minute review after the second daily sync to confirm data stability.

## What to measure

- Share of keywords in positions 1–3 / 4–10 / 11–20 after day 0 vs day 14
- Top pages contributing ≥70% of organic clicks
- Competitor domains appearing on ≥30% of your tracked keywords
- Country mix shifts >5 percentage points after catalog or market launches
