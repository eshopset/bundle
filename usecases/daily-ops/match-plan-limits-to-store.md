## Why limits are a use case

Outgrowing a plan mid-promotion means blocked keyword adds or truncated sheet syncs. Matching limits to catalog size and content ambition is part of daily ops—not an annual finance-only decision.

## How it works in EShopSet

Compare limits on the [pricing](https://eshopset.com/pricing/) page:

- **SEO Performance Monitor:** 25 / 50 / 100 keywords · 5 / 10 / 20 competitors · 10 / 20 / 50 top pages
- **AI Presence:** 10 / 50 / 100 keywords
- **Data Sync:** 1,000 rows (Starter) · 5,000 (Growth/Pro); hourly schedules from Growth

## Example: right-sizing

A store with 3,400 active SKUs and 60 SEO keywords sat uncomfortably on Starter (1k sheet rows, 25 SEO keywords). Moving to Growth unlocked 5k rows and 50 SEO / 50 AI keywords—enough for a Q4 assortment without fragmenting across tools. At $99 launch vs stitching separate SEO + sheet tools, they estimated ~40–60% lower tooling spend.

## Playbook

1. Count active SKUs / sheet rows needed.
2. Count SEO + AI keywords you truly review monthly.
3. Pick the lowest plan that fits with ~20% headroom.
4. Revisit before BFCM or major catalog expansions.
5. Don’t buy Pro “just in case” if Growth covers 5k rows and 50/50 keywords.

## What to measure

- % of plan caps utilized
- Blocked actions due to limits (should be rare)
- Cost vs prior point solutions
