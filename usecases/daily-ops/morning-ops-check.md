_Run a 10–15 minute morning Shopify ops check across SEO Performance Monitor, AI Presence, and Data Sync Overviews—last sync, KPIs, and errors._

## Outcomes

- 10–15 minutes across three Overviews
- Confirm last sync time, KPI direction, and obvious errors
- Escalate to Logs only when something looks off

## Why a ritual beats inbox-driven ops

Without a checklist, teams only notice SEO or sync failures when revenue dips. A morning pass catches stale syncs, citation cliffs, and sheet apply errors while there’s still time to fix before peak traffic.

## How it works in EShopSet

Open Overview for each app. Verify last sync timestamps, scan KPI sparklines/tables, and note anomalies. Dive into Logs only for failures or partial runs. Keep a shared standup note: green / yellow / red per app.

## Example: three-flag morning

SEO: green (sync 6:02am). AI Presence: yellow (Perplexity run lagged). Data Sync: red (12 row errors on SKU typos). Ops fixed the sheet by 9:15am and re-ran manual preview/apply—before the noon promo email.

## Playbook

1. SEO Overview: sync time + any ≥3 position cluster moves.
2. AI Presence: citation/mention direction + model outages.
3. Data Sync: last schedule result + error count.
4. Log anything yellow/red with owner.
5. Once a week, extend into the deeper weekly SEO/AI reviews.

## What to measure

- % of days all three apps green
- Mean time to resolve red flags
- Incidents caught in morning vs reported by customers

## Proof points

- **10–15 min** — Daily ritual
- **3 Overviews** — SEO · AI · Sheets
- **<24h** — Max lag before escalate
