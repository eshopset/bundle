## Outcomes

- One catalog → three apps for SEO, AI presence, and sheet sync

## Why enable all three early

Merchants who only install one app delay the operating rhythm. SEO, AI answers, and catalog truth interact—price changes affect availability messaging; content changes affect rankings and AI citations. Enabling the bundle from day one keeps those loops in one place.

## How it works in EShopSet

From the [apps catalog](https://eshopset.com/apps/), enable SEO Performance Monitor, AI Presence, and Data Sync on your connected Shopify store. Open each app’s Settings for domain, brand/keywords, and sheet mapping. Confirm plan limits on [pricing](https://eshopset.com/pricing/).

## Example: new Growth subscriber

A 1,200-SKU store enabled all three in ~25 minutes: SEO domain + 30 keywords, AI Presence brand seed (20 queries), Data Sync connected with inventory mapping. By end of day they had first SEO/AI syncs queued and a sheet preview ready for morning apply.

## Playbook

1. Install Now / connect Shopify.
2. Enable the three apps from the catalog.
3. Complete Settings for each (don’t leave defaults blank).
4. Run or schedule first syncs.
5. Bookmark Overview links for the morning ops check.
