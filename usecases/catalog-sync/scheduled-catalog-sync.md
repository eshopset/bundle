**scheduled Shopify catalog sync** keeps supplier sheets and Shopify catalogs aligned without CSV upload roulette. [Data Sync](https://eshopset.com/apps/data-sync/) maps columns, previews creates vs updates, then applies—once or on a schedule—inside the same EShopSet plan as SEO and AI Presence.

_Schedule Data Sync hourly, daily, or weekly so Shopify price and inventory stay aligned with your Google Sheet—up to 5,000 rows on Growth/Pro._

## Outcomes

- Hourly / daily / weekly cadences on Growth and Pro
- Starter supports manual + daily for smaller catalogs
- Pair schedules with preview-on-manual for risky changes

## Why schedules beat ad-hoc CSVs

Promotion calendars and 3PL inventory feeds change faster than Admin uploads. A daily or hourly Data Sync job reduces “site says in stock / sheet says 0” incidents that create support tickets and cancelled orders.

## How it works in EShopSet

After a successful one-time import, set schedule in Data Sync Settings. Growth/Pro unlock hourly/daily/weekly. Use price/inventory-only policies when editorial fields should stay in Shopify. Review Logs each morning.

## Example: flash-sale week

During a 5-day flash sale, a merchant ran **hourly** syncs on ~2,200 rows. Inventory accuracy issues reported to support dropped from ~15 tickets/day to 2–3, mostly edge-case variants excluded from the sheet.

## Playbook

1. Prove mapping with a one-time import.
2. Choose cadence from change frequency (inventory-heavy → hourly/daily).
3. Enable schedule; document owner for Log review.
4. Pause schedule before massive structural sheet redesigns.
5. Resume after a manual preview/apply succeeds.

## What to measure

- Sync success rate over 7 days
- Support tickets tagged inventory/price mismatch
- Average time from sheet edit → live Shopify

## Proof points

- **Hourly→Weekly** — Cadence options
- **≤5,000** — Rows on Growth/Pro
