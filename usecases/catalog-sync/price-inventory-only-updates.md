**Shopify price inventory sheet sync** keeps supplier sheets and Shopify catalogs aligned without CSV upload roulette. [Data Sync](https://eshopset.com/apps/data-sync/) maps columns, previews creates vs updates, then applies—once or on a schedule—inside the same EShopSet plan as SEO and AI Presence.

_Set Data Sync matched-row policy to price and inventory only so Shopify merchandising fields stay untouched during Google Sheet syncs._

## Outcomes

- Protect titles, descriptions, and images from sheet overwrites
- Still move price and inventory on schedule
- Ideal for marketing-led catalogs with ops-owned stock sheets

## Why a narrow policy exists

Full-row syncs are powerful and dangerous. A supplier sheet rarely has better product storytelling than your brand team. Price/inventory-only policy encodes that organizational truth into Data Sync.

## How it works in EShopSet

In Data Sync Settings, set matched-row policy to update price and inventory only. Map those columns; keep content columns out of the apply path. Preview should show updates without content-field churn.

## Example: agency + merchant split

An agency wrote PDP copy in Shopify; the merchant updated a 1,800-row cost/stock sheet daily. After switching to price/inventory-only, zero copy regressions appeared in weekly QA (previously ~20–40 PDPs/week needed rollbacks).

## Playbook

1. Confirm who owns copy vs commerce facts.
2. Enable price/inventory-only for matched rows.
3. Run preview; verify no title/body changes listed.
4. Apply; spot-check 5 PDPs for copy integrity and 5 for price accuracy.
5. Keep a separate process for rare content imports.

## What to measure

- Content rollback tickets after sync
- Price/inventory accuracy vs sheet

## Proof points

- **Price + stock** — Synced fields
- **Merchandising** — Stays in Shopify
- **Fewer tickets** — From overwritten copy
