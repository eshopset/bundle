## Why citations ≠ brand vanity

A mention without a usable citation rarely converts. Citation rate tells you whether answer engines treat you as a source. Competing domains reveal whether publishers, retailers, or marketplaces own the narrative for your category prompts.

## How it works in EShopSet

AI Presence Overview charts citation rate and mentions by model for your tracked keywords, with tables for competing domains. Use Logs after sync if a model run fails so gaps aren’t mistaken for “zero presence.”

## Example: skincare citations

A skincare brand tracked 40 Growth-plan keywords. Brand prompts cited them on 3/4 models (~65% citation rate), but “best retinol for beginners” cited two publishers and one competitor on all four models. They published a beginner guide with clear ingredients table and clinical-sounding FAQs; within ~3 weeks citation rate on that cluster rose from 0% → ~40% on two models.

## Playbook

1. Segment keywords: brand, category, comparison.
2. Record citation rate per segment weekly.
3. List top competing domains per segment.
4. Brief content that can realistically earn citations (guides, data, unique specs)—not thin PDPs alone.
5. Re-check after content ships across 5–10 syncs.

## What to measure

- Citation rate overall and by model
- Mention share vs top 3 competing domains
- Queries with mentions but zero citations
