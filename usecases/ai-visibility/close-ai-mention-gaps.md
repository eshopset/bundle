## Outcomes

- Gap list from per-keyword competitor visibility
- Trend lines show whether gaps are widening

## Why closing gaps beats spraying content

Publishing 20 new posts won’t help if AI answers already prefer three authoritative URLs. Closing gaps means matching the evidence those answers cite—unique data, clearer specs, fresher guides—on the exact prompts where rivals appear and you don’t.

## How it works in EShopSet

Use Overview trends plus per-keyword tables in AI Presence to list queries where competing domains appear without your brand. Sort by commercial intent, then assign owners. Pair with SEO Performance Monitor when the same themes underperform in classic search.

## Example: pet food brand

On 12 comparison keywords, Competitor A appeared in 4/4 models; the brand appeared in 0–1. They shipped one head-to-head guide with feeding tables and third-party test notes. After ~18 days, they appeared in 2/4 models on 7 of those 12 queries—enough to justify a second round of SKU-level FAQs.

## Playbook

1. Filter keywords: competitor mentioned, brand not.
2. Score by revenue potential (collection/SKU mapping).
3. For each top gap, open 1–2 AI answers manually and note cited sources.
4. Brief content that can earn a citation (not a thin rewrite).
5. Re-check the gap list after 10–15 syncs.

## What to measure

- Count of gap keywords week over week
- % of gap keywords closed (brand now mentioned)
- Models where gaps persist longest
