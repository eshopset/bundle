_Freelancers: set up AI Presence on each client Shopify store to track brand citations in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and AI Overviews—billed per store._

## Outcomes

- Lightweight per-client setup: brand, aliases, seed prompts
- Report visibility score and model mix from Overview
- No shared freelancers “master account” for all clients required

## Why freelancers track AI per client

Clients ask “are we showing up in ChatGPT?” Answering needs that client’s domain and brand strings—not a blended portfolio view.

## How it works in EShopSet

Enable AI Presence on the client store, configure Settings, let daily sync fill Overview, and screenshot citation trends for deliverables. Keep each client on the plan that fits keyword volume.

## Example: two boutique clients

A freelancer ran Starter on Client A (15 prompts) and Growth on Client B (40 prompts). Monthly reports compared citation rate and competing domains—Clients never saw each other’s data.

## Playbook

1. Confirm brand spelling and aliases with the client.
2. Enable AI Presence; seed prompts.
3. Wait for first successful sync; verify Logs.
4. Monthly: Overview metrics into the client report.
5. Adjust keywords when campaigns change.

## What to measure

- Citation rate MoM per client
- Prompt coverage vs plan cap
- Deliverables using same-week Overview data

## Proof points
