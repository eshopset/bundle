**multi-store Shopify AI presence** means each Shopify store keeps its own plan, app enables, and usage limits—not a shared multi-store control center. This use case shows how in-house teams, agencies, and freelancers run aI presence checks across brand stores store by store with EShopSet.

_In-house multi-brand teams: track ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and AI Overviews citations per Shopify store with AI Presence—separate plans and keyword caps per store._

## Outcomes

- Separate AI Presence Settings (brand, aliases, keywords) per store
- Track ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews—not Claude
- Compare citation rate across brands without a control-center product

## Why AI visibility is per brand

Models cite domains and brand strings. A portfolio that shares one keyword list or one install cannot tell which storefront is missing from “best X for Y” answers. Per-store tracking keeps owners accountable.

## How it works in EShopSet

On each store, enable AI Presence, set brand/domain/aliases and seed queries up to the plan keyword cap, then review Overview for visibility score, citation rate, and competing domains. Repeat for every brand store on its own plan.

## Example: beauty + wellness pair

Store Wellness ranked in Perplexity for “clean serum,” while Store Beauty had zero ChatGPT citations on the same theme. The in-house content team reused Wellness’s winning phrasing on Beauty’s blog—then watched AI Presence on Beauty’s Overview the following week.

## Playbook

1. Inventory brand names and aliases per store.
2. Enable AI Presence; seed 10–20 category prompts per store.
3. Log visibility score + citation rate weekly per store.
4. Share wins across brands; keep Settings isolated.
5. Escalate model-specific gaps via Logs if syncs fail.

## What to measure

- Citation rate trend per store
- Share of mentions by model
- Competing domains appearing more often than your brand
