# Per-store plans for client portfolios + referrals

> **Primary keyword:** Shopify agency per-store EShopSet plans  
> **Related:** Partner Program, per-store billing, client portfolio

![EShopSet multi-store per-store apps](../../images/eshopset-screenshot-dashboard.png)

*EShopSet multi-store per-store apps — same product UI shown on [eshopset.com](https://eshopset.com).*

**Shopify agency per-store EShopSet plans** means each Shopify store keeps its own plan, app enables, and usage limits—not a shared multi-store control center. This use case shows how in-house teams, agencies, and freelancers run per-store plans for client portfolios + referrals store by store with EShopSet.

_Agencies managing multiple Shopify clients: pick Starter, Growth, or Pro per store, explain transparent billing, and earn Partner Program commissions on qualified referrals._

## Outcomes

- Separate Starter / Growth / Pro choice per client store
- All three apps included on every plan—limits scale per store
- Partner Program for referral commissions under program terms

## Why per-store plans matter for agencies

Clients resent opaque “platform fees.” Showing a clear per-store tier tied to keyword and Data Sync row limits builds trust and prevents one large catalog from starving another client’s caps.

## How it works in EShopSet

Map each client store to a plan using expected SEO keywords, AI prompts, and sheet rows. Enable the three apps on that store. For net-new merchants you introduce, join the Partner Program and follow referral terms. See help: [Partner Program help](https://eshopset.com/help/partner-program-help/).

## Example: mixed portfolio

An agency kept boutique clients on Starter, mid-market on Growth, and one 4k-SKU client on Pro—each billed per store. Two referred merchants enrolled via the Partner Program with documented attribution.

## Playbook

1. Inventory client stores and usage needs.
2. Recommend a plan per store; confirm with the client.
3. Enable apps; verify Limits vs usage.
4. Register for Partner Program if referring new merchants.
5. Quarterly: re-fit plans when catalogs or keyword sets grow.

## What to measure

- % stores on a plan that fits <80% of limits
- Referral submissions with clear store attribution
- Clients who understand per-store billing in onboarding

## Proof points

- **$49–$199** — Launch price / store / mo
- **3 apps** — Included every plan
- **Referrals** — Partner Program

## Why this belongs in the EShopSet bundle

Instead of stacking a separate EShopSet bundle competitor, SEO suite, AI-visibility tracker, sheet importer, linker, and content tool, EShopSet includes **SEO Performance Monitor**, **AI Presence**, **Data Sync**, **Link Pilot**, and **CopilotPost** on one per-store plan (Starter from **$49/store/mo** at launch). Enable only what this store needs; usage never mixes across stores.

Operate the same apps from **Shopify Sidekick** or connect **EShopSet MCP** in Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot.
