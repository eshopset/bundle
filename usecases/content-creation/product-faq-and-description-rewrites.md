## Why PDP copy still matters

Shoppers and crawlers need clear descriptions and FAQs on the product. Thin or empty fields waste ads traffic and leave questions unanswered on the page.

## How it works in CopilotPost

Catalogue scan flags rewrite actions (description, FAQ, title/meta). Accept → free brief → generate draft (1 credit for FAQ/description/SEO metadata). Review in Content, then publish to the product.Filter Opportunities for FAQ and description rewrites.

## Example: bestsellers with empty FAQs

Ten bestsellers lack FAQ metafields. CopilotPost queues FAQ opportunities from reviews and manual questions. The merchant drafts five at 1 credit each and publishes after preview.

1. Filter Opportunities for product rewrite / FAQ actions.
2. Prioritize revenue PDPs.
3. Accept, brief, draft (watch credit cost).
4. Preview and publish within the weekly cap.
5. Re-index later to confirm gaps closed.
