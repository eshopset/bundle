**Shopify content opportunities evidence** should start from catalogue and demand evidence—not a blank prompt. [CopilotPost](https://eshopset.com/apps/copilotpost/) ranks opportunities, unlocks free briefs, then credit-priced drafts you publish with approval into Shopify.

_Connect catalogue and demand sources in CopilotPost, find ranked opportunities with evidence, and accept top items into free briefs._

## Outcomes

- Ranked opportunities with stored evidence (why this ranks)
- Catalogue gaps plus GSC, reviews, People also ask, and manual questions
- Accept to unlock a free brief before you spend credits on a draft

## Why evidence beats blank-page blogging

Generic “write a blog” tools ignore your catalogue and search demand. Thin descriptions, missing FAQs, and unanswered queries pile up. Opportunities with evidence show merchants what to write next—and why—before credits are spent.

## How it works in CopilotPost

After catalogue index, open **Opportunities** (or run **Find opportunities**). Each row includes evidence from sources you enabled. Accept an item to create a free brief; skip or reopen as needed. Rules-first scoring keeps rankings explainable.Evidence explains why the opportunity ranked.

## Example: collection without a guide

A skincare brand indexes collections missing buying guides. CopilotPost ranks a “Winter barrier routine” opportunity with catalogue matches and related questions. The merchant accepts, reviews the free brief, then drafts when ready.

## Playbook

1. Install EShopSet and enable CopilotPost; finish catalogue sync.
2. Connect Sources you care about (GSC CSV is available on all plans).
3. Run **Find opportunities**; sort by score.
4. Open evidence; accept 3–5 high-intent items.
5. Continue in Content with free briefs.

## What to measure

- Opportunities accepted per week
- % of accepts that reach published
- Coverage of thin catalogue gaps

## Proof points

- **Free** — Opportunities + briefs
- **Evidence** — Why each idea ranks
- **Approve** — Human publish gate
