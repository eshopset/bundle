**Shopify weekly content plan** should start from catalogue and demand evidence—not a blank prompt. [CopilotPost](https://eshopset.com/apps/copilotpost/) ranks opportunities, unlocks free briefs, then credit-priced drafts you publish with approval into Shopify.

_Use CopilotPost Home week plan to slot Mon–Sun content from real store gaps—free briefs first, drafts when ready._

## Outcomes

- Post-install / post-discovery 7-day week plan
- Slots tied to opportunities and content stages
- Free briefs before you spend draft credits

## Why a store-grounded week plan works

Calendars fail when topics ignore the catalogue. A week plan built from opportunities keeps daily work tied to evidence and capacity (credits + weekly publish cap).

## How it works in CopilotPost

Home shows the week plan after discovery. Open slots, advance briefs, draft when credits allow, and respect the weekly publish cap. Re-run discovery when the catalogue changes.Accept opportunities when a week-plan slot needs work.

## Example: agency client week

An agency installs CopilotPost per client store. After scan, Home proposes seven slots. They brief all seven (free), draft three articles mid-week, and publish two under the cap.

## Playbook

1. Finish onboarding scan and first opportunity run.
2. Open Home week plan; confirm Mon–Sun slots.
3. Accept related opportunities if a slot is empty.
4. Brief everything; draft only what you will publish this week.
5. Publish within the weekly cap; roll leftovers.

## What to measure

- Slots completed vs planned
- Credits used vs monthly allotment
- Publishes vs weekly cap

## Proof points

- **7 days** — Week plan slots
- **Free** — Briefs first
- **Cap** — Weekly publish limit
