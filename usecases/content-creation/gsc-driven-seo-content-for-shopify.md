_Import Google Search Console queries or connect live GSC in CopilotPost to create keyword-driven content opportunities and drafts._

## Outcomes

- GSC CSV import on all plans; live GSC on Starter+
- Opportunities from queries and pages you already almost win
- Route to article, update article, or other content actions

## Why GSC beats guesswork

Search Console shows queries and pages with impressions but weak CTR or position. Content that answers those queries with your catalogue converts research into revenue paths.

## How it works in CopilotPost

Settings → Sources: upload Queries CSV or connect live GSC (Starter+). Find opportunities; accept high-intent rows; brief and draft. Publish articles or update existing posts.After import or live sync, Find opportunities from query demand.

## Example: positions 8–15

A home brand imports Queries.csv, filters commercial phrases in positions 8–15, and accepts five opportunities. Articles draft at 3 credits each; they publish two in the first week under the weekly cap.

## Playbook

1. Export GSC Performance → Queries (or connect live GSC).
2. Import or sync in Sources.
3. Find opportunities; prioritize commercial intent.
4. Accept → brief → draft → publish.
5. Re-check GSC after 14–28 days.

## What to measure

- GSC position/CTR for targeted queries
- Published pieces from GSC opportunities
- Analytics on CopilotPost-published URLs

## Proof points
