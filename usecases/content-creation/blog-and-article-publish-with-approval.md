_Review CopilotPost drafts, use Page Preview, then publish or schedule to Shopify within the weekly cap—human approval required._

## Outcomes

- Page Preview for assembled article/page drafts
- Publish now or schedule; revert published changes
- Human approval required; weekly cap cannot be turned off

## Why approval beats autopilot

Brand, claims, and SEO quality need a human gate. CopilotPost drafts fast; you approve what goes live within a weekly publish cap.

## How it works in CopilotPost

Content queue: brief → draft → scheduled → published. Open a draft, use Page Preview, then Publish now or schedule. Automation may draft or queue, but publish still needs approval. Default weekly cap is 5 (Pro can raise up to 20).Page Preview is available for assembled article and page drafts.

## Example: two articles this week

Three drafts are ready. The merchant previews all three, publishes two today, schedules one for Thursday, and hits the weekly cap intentionally—no surprise posts.

## Playbook

1. Open Content → Drafts.
2. Edit for Brand rules and facts.
3. Page Preview; fix issues.
4. Publish or schedule within remaining weekly cap.
5. Use revert if a publish needs undoing.

## What to measure

- Publishes per week vs cap
- Revert rate
- Time from draft to publish

## Proof points

- **Default 5** — Weekly publish cap
- **Pro ≤20** — Higher cap option
- **Approval** — Always required
